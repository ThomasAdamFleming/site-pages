Privacy Policy for Tom's Assistant This application is a private, single-user productivity assistant built and used solely by Tom Fleming. It is not a commercial product and has no other users or customers. Data accessed: with explicit Google OAuth authorisation, this application can access Gmail (read, send, organise email), Google Calendar (view and create events), and Google Drive/Docs/Sheets/Slides (read, create, edit files) belonging solely to the account holder. How data is used: solely to carry out actions the account holder requests in real time through direct conversation with the assistant - e.g. summarising an email, drafting a reply, checking a calendar, editing a document. No other use. Data sharing: never sold, rented, or shared with any third party, advertiser, or other service. Data retention: nothing is stored beyond what's needed to complete a requested action; no separate database of the account holder's data is kept. Access revocation: the account holder can revoke access anytime at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Contact: tomfleming+legal@gmail.com with any questions.
